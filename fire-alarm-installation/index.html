Fire alarm installation for commercial and domestic premises

A fire alarm system only does its job if it is designed around the building it protects. Ignis Secure Limited surveys your premises first, then designs and installs a fire detection and alarm system that reflects the layout, the way the building is used and the findings of your fire risk assessment. We work with reference to BS 5839, the British Standard for fire detection and fire alarm systems, and we hand over the documentation you need for your own records.

We install new systems, replace ageing ones and extend existing systems when buildings change. Our clients across West London include offices, shops, restaurants, schools, warehouses, landlords with HMO properties and homeowners who want detection beyond a basic smoke alarm.

What our fire alarm installation service includes

  • A free site survey and a written, itemised quotation
  • System design with reference to BS 5839 and your fire risk assessment
  • Supply and installation of panels, detectors, call points and sounders
  • Neat cabling, or wireless equipment where cabling is impractical
  • Commissioning, cause and effect testing, and a commissioning certificate
  • A walk through handover so your staff know how the system works
  • Logbook setup and clear guidance on routine testing and servicing

Types of fire alarm system we install

Conventional systems

A cost effective choice for smaller premises. The building is divided into zones, and the panel tells you which zone an alarm has come from. Well suited to small offices, shops and similar units.

Addressable systems

Each device has its own address, so the panel identifies the exact detector or call point that triggered. Addressable systems suit larger or more complex buildings where finding the source quickly matters, and they make fault finding and servicing faster.

Wireless systems

Radio linked devices that avoid long cable runs. Useful in finished buildings where cabling would be disruptive, in temporary setups, and in buildings where the fabric should not be disturbed.

Domestic systems

For houses, flats and HMOs we install systems with reference to BS 5839-6, which covers fire detection in dwellings, including the grades and categories typically requested by local authorities for licensed HMOs.

Who we install fire alarms for

Most of our installation work falls into a few familiar building types, each with its own requirements:

  • Offices and commercial units, where the system needs to fit around working hours and existing fit outs.
  • Shops and restaurants, where kitchens, storerooms and public areas each need the right type of detection.
  • HMOs and rented properties, where landlords need systems that satisfy licensing conditions and keep tenants safe.
  • Schools and community buildings, where phased work and clear communication around term times matter.
  • Warehouses and industrial units, where high ceilings and dust call for carefully chosen detection.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a fire alarm installation take?

It depends on the size of the building and the type of system. A small office or shop can often be completed in one to two days, while larger or multi floor premises take longer. We confirm a realistic timescale in your quotation and agree working hours that suit you.

Does my business legally need a fire alarm?

In England and Wales the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 requires the responsible person for a non domestic premises to have appropriate fire detection and warning arrangements in place. What is appropriate depends on your fire risk assessment. We can survey your premises and explain what your assessment is likely to call for, but the fire risk assessment itself remains the deciding document.

Will the installation disrupt my business?

We plan installations to keep disruption to a minimum. That can mean working outside your opening hours, phasing the work area by area, or using wireless equipment where running cables would be intrusive. We agree the approach with you before work starts.

Can you extend or take over an existing fire alarm system?

In many cases, yes. If your existing panel and devices are serviceable we can often extend the system to cover new areas, replace faulty devices or take over routine servicing. We start with an inspection of the current system and give you an honest view on whether extending it or replacing it is the better value option.

What category of fire alarm system do I need?

BS 5839-1 describes categories of system for non domestic buildings, from category M manual systems through to L1, which provides automatic detection throughout the building. The right category for your premises is normally identified by your fire risk assessment. During the site survey we explain the options in plain English and design the system to match.
