Fire alarm installers serving Uxbridge

Uxbridge has an unusually wide mix of buildings for one town. The High Street and the shopping centres bring together retail units, restaurants and offices above shops. The business parks on the edge of town house large modern offices, while the streets around Brunel University include a high concentration of shared houses and student lets. Each of these calls for a different approach to fire detection, and that is exactly the range of work we handle.

Ignis Secure Limited is based in West London, so Uxbridge is home ground rather than the far end of a coverage map. We design, install and commission fire alarm systems with reference to BS 5839, and we hand over the certificates and logbook you need for your records. Our full fire alarm installation service page explains the system types in more detail.

Buildings we work in around Uxbridge

  • Town centre shops and restaurants, where we plan work around trading hours and design detection that copes with kitchens and storerooms.
  • Offices above retail on the High Street, which often need shared escape routes covered properly and clear zoning between units.
  • HMOs and student housing near Brunel University, installed with reference to BS 5839-6 and the conditions in Hillingdon HMO licences.
  • Business park offices, including premises at Stockley Park and Uxbridge Business Park, where addressable systems and tidy out of hours working are usually the right fit.
  • Schools and community buildings across UB8 and UB10, where we phase work around term dates and building use.
  • Industrial units around the Cowley Mill Road area, where ceiling heights and dust affect detector choice.

Why use a local installer in Uxbridge?

Being nearby changes how the job runs. Site surveys are easy to arrange, engineers are not travelling across the city to reach you, and if something needs a second visit it happens quickly. It also means we already know the building stock: the Victorian shop conversions in the old town, the post war housing in Cowley and Hillingdon, and the modern office space on the business parks all behave differently when you are designing detection and running cables.

If a fire risk assessment, an insurance review or an HMO licensing inspection has flagged your fire alarm arrangements, we can survey the premises, explain what the finding means in practice and quote for putting it right.

What happens when you get in touch

The process is the same as for all our installation work. We visit your premises for a free survey, talk through what your fire risk assessment calls for, and send a written quotation. Once you go ahead, we install, commission and certificate the system, then walk you through how it works. After handover we can also take care of routine servicing, and our guide on how often a fire alarm should be serviced sets out what to expect.

Do you cover the whole UB8 and UB10 area?
