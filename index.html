Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in West London and carry out fire alarm, CCTV, access control and electrical work across Uxbridge, Hillingdon, Hayes, Ruislip, Harrow, Ealing and the surrounding areas. If you are nearby but not sure whether we cover you, give us a call and we will let you know.

Do you work with both businesses and homeowners?

Yes. We work with commercial clients such as offices, shops, schools, landlords and managing agents, as well as homeowners and residential landlords. Every system is designed around the building and how it is used.

Are your fire alarm systems designed to British Standards?

Our fire alarm systems are designed, installed and commissioned with reference to BS 5839, the British Standard covering fire detection and alarm systems for buildings. We talk you through the recommended category of system for your premises before any work begins.

How do I get a quote?

Call us or send the enquiry form on this page. For most projects we arrange a free site survey first, so the quotation reflects your actual building rather than a guess. There is no obligation attached to a survey or a quote.

Do you offer ongoing servicing and maintenance?

Yes. As well as new installations we look after existing fire alarm systems with routine servicing, testing and repairs. Ask us about a maintenance arrangement that suits your premises when you get in touch. You can also read our guide on how often a fire alarm should be serviced.
